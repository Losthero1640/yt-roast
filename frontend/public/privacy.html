Zero-Persistence Data Policy

Privacy Policy for YouTube Roast AI

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Core Commitment: YouTube Roast AI was engineered from day one with zero persistent databases, zero temporary file caching, and zero user data harvesting. Your viewing history and OAuth credentials are used ephemerally in RAM and purged immediately upon report generation.

1. What Data We Access

When you choose to use Quick Roast (Google OAuth), with your explicit consent we request read-only access to:

  • Your public YouTube channel subscriptions
  • Your liked YouTube videos

We do not access your private Google account details, passwords, payment info, search queries, or watch history outside the authorized public YouTube endpoints.

2. How We Use and Process Data

  • In-Memory Processing: Data fetched from Google's API or uploaded via Google Takeout is streamed directly into volatile system memory (RAM).
  • AI Synthesis: Anonymized viewing statistics (aggregate topic counts and creator frequencies) are sent to Google Gemini to generate your humorous roast narrative.
  • Zero Database Storage: We do not operate any database (no MongoDB, SQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, or cloud storage). No personal records are stored on our servers.
  • Ephemeral Tokens: The temporary OAuth access token provided by Google is held in transient memory only for the seconds required to fetch your data, after which it is discarded.

3. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties or advertisers. We transmit only aggregate viewing metrics to Google Gemini for AI report generation under strict confidentiality guidelines.

4. Revoking Access

You can revoke YouTube Roast AI's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Security settings: Google Account Permissions.
