<task id="02-01-03" wave="1">
<title>Implement size-capped, PII-purged prompt builder with hard 4KB measurement and rejection</title>
<description>
Create `src/ai/prompt-builder.js` exporting:
1. `buildRoastPrompt({ profile, scoreResult })`:
   - Enforces evidence caps:
     - Top 5 primary creators (name, count, share %) + ranks 6–15 secondary creators (names only).
     - Top 5 repeated titles (count >= 3) + overall replay ratio.
     - Top 5 late-night titles with derived hour strings (`"watched around 2 AM"`) rather than exact timestamps.
     - Up to 8 candidate topics.
     - Locked deterministic Brainrot score, tier, confidence, and subscores.
   - Defense against prompt bloat:
     - Clamps individual title strings and creator names to max 80 characters.
     - Hard serialization measurement & rejection: Measures `Buffer.byteLength(prompt, 'utf8')`. If the assembled text exceeds 4,096 bytes even after initial clamping, the builder immediately throws `PayloadTooLargeError` (`code: 'PROMPT_TOO_LARGE'`), rejecting the payload before any network call.
   - Privacy purging (`PRIV-03`):
     - Completely strips Google account IDs, emails, profile names.
     - Completely strips video URLs (`titleUrl`), channel URLs, client IP addresses.
     - Purges exact ISO timestamps (only relative spans and derived hour strings are included).
     - Never includes raw bulk logs.
   - System instructions:
     - Enforces witty, savage, dry observational comedic voice.
     - Mandates at least 4 jokes covering all 4 styles (`pattern`, `combination`, `specific`, `archetype`).
     - Mandates that combination jokes synthesize at least 2 distinct patterns.
     - Strictly forbids inventing creators, titles, or habits not present in the supplied evidence.
     - Forbids clinical psychiatric/medical assertions (e.g. converting insomnia/ADHD into satirical behavioral critique).
     - Injects locked deterministic score and tier as unalterable ground truth.
2. `extractAllowedEvidence(profile)`: Helper returning sets of valid creator names and video titles for post-generation grounding verification.
3. Test suite in `tests/unit/prompt-builder.test.js`:
   - Malicious/malformed strings: Verifies resilience against HTML injection, script tags, and unusual unicode without prompt corruption.
   - URL stripping: Asserts prompt contains zero URLs (`http://`, `https://`, `youtube.com`).
   - Identifier leakage: Asserts zero Google IDs, email addresses, or IP addresses appear in output.
   - Oversized payload rejection: Asserts that payloads exceeding 4,096 bytes are measured and rejected with `PayloadTooLargeError`.
   - Title clamping: Asserts titles > 80 characters are safely clamped.
   - Score injection: Asserts deterministic score, tier, and subscores are accurately represented.
</description>
